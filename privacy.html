Heywhen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Heywhen helps you plan your time, see your earnings and make plans with friends. We collect as little as we can. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who we are

Heywhen is an app made by Dhanushka Lakshman Francisku Baduge (“we”, “us”), based in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR).

Contact: heywhen.help@outlook.com

Information that stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device. We cannot see them:

  • Your shifts, planned and actual hours, pay rates and earnings
  • Goals, expenses, budgets and your private shopping list
  • Rota photos and PDFs you import. Heywhen reads them on your phone; they are not uploaded.
  • Morning motivation settings. Notifications are made on your phone.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted too. You can save a backup file in Settings.

Information we keep when you make an account

To use friends, Circles and chat, you make a Heywhen account. We keep:

  • Account: your email address and password (stored safely, never readable by us).
  • Profile: your name, @username, short line and profile photo, if you add them.
  • Social: friend requests, friends, Circles, Circle chat messages, the free time you choose to share, shared shopping lists, and people you block.
  • Safety reports: if you report a person or message, we keep the report so we can check it.

Your friends and people in your Circles can see your profile, your messages in that Circle and the free time you share with them. Anyone with a shopping list link can see the items on that list.

Why we use it

  • To give you the service you asked for: your account, friends, Circles, chat and shared lists (contract).
  • To keep Heywhen safe, for example checking reports and stopping abuse (legitimate interests).
  • To answer you when you contact us (legitimate interests).
  • Notifications and camera or photo access are only used if you allow them in your iPhone settings (consent). You can change this at any time.

Who helps us

We use Supabase to store account and social information securely. They act only on our instructions. Information may be processed outside the UK; when it is, it is protected by approved safeguards such as standard contract clauses.

If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. We do not see your card details.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, photo, friends, Circles you made, your messages, shared free time and shared lists are deleted. Reports about safety may be kept without your name for up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account any time in the app: Profile → Delete my account, or Settings → Help & privacy → Delete my account.

To use your other rights, email us. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Heywhen is for people aged 13 and over. If you think a child under 13 has an account, please tell us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is sent over secure (encrypted) connections. Access to account data is limited by strict rules, so people only see what they are allowed to see.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is important.
