Help & contact

Need help? Email us at heywhen.help@outlook.com. We usually reply within 2 days.

Common questions

How do I add my shifts?

On the Today page, tap Import rota to read a photo or PDF of your rota, or tap + to add a shift yourself.

How do I change my pay period?

Go to Insights → Pay period and tap Update pay period. Choose the start and end dates, check the preview and save. Old pay periods keep their records.

How do I add friends and make a Circle?

Tap the Circles tab. Search for a friend’s @username and send a request. When they accept, make a Circle and add them.

How do I shop together?

Open Shopping list and tap Shop together with a Circle. Everyone in the Circle can add and tick items.

How do I turn morning messages on or off?

Go to Settings → Morning motivation. You can choose the time or turn it off.

How do I report or block someone?

In a Circle chat, tap a message and choose Report, or open the person and choose Block. We check every report within 24 hours.

I got a new phone. Where is my data?

Shifts, earnings, goals and expenses are saved on your phone. Before you change phones, go to Settings → Save a backup, then use Restore backup on the new phone. Your account, friends and Circles come back when you log in.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Help & privacy → Delete my account (or Profile → Delete my account). Type DELETE to confirm. This cannot be undone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Heywhen and tap Cancel.
